Engaging Local 801 is the private work application for approved MAPE Local 801 users.

Authorized users can coordinate member engagement, campaigns, CAT actions, follow-ups, documents, notifications, and secure web reports according to their assigned role. Microsoft Entra ID and multi-factor authentication verify identity, while Local 801 administrators control application access and roles.

Mobile capabilities include secure document and QR scanning, device biometric confirmation, generic calendar reminders, notifications, and protected background uploads. Protected member records are not stored for offline browsing.

An approved Local 801 account is required. The application does not provide public registration and cannot be used with an unapproved Microsoft account.
